- **New HOMEBREW tab.** Browse more than 1,600 homebrew games, demos and tools for the Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance and NES from [Homebrew Hub](https://hh.gbdev.io), the archive behind gbdev.io, gbadev.org and nesdev.org. The newest additions are at the top, and X switches between All, GBA, GBC, GB and NES.
- **One-button installs into the right folder.** Press A on a game and Mixtape saves the ROM into OnionOS's folder for that system (`Roms/GBA`, `Roms/GBC`, `Roms/GB` or `Roms/FC`), named after the game, with its cover added as box art. It shows up in Games right away. X erases it again; your saves are kept.
- **Live from Homebrew Hub.** The list comes from Homebrew Hub's public API. The first time you open the tab, Mixtape downloads the whole list in the background (about a minute) and keeps a copy on the card. After that it only checks for what's new, and Y syncs again at any time. In the details screen, Y flips through the game's screenshots.
- ROM hacks of commercial games aren't listed.

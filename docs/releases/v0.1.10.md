- **A Mixtape theme for OnionOS.** `Mixtape-Theme-v0.1.10.zip` (attached below) reskins OnionOS to match Mixtape: amber phosphor on black, scanlines and the amber/magenta/cyan tape stripes, with a cassette on the Games tile, amber highlight bars in every list, LED-meter brightness and a tape-reel charging animation. Copy its `Themes` folder to your SD card, then pick **Mixtape** in Apps → Theme Switcher. It keeps OnionOS's layout and your icon pack.
- **Mixtape in Games (optional).** SELECT now opens a small Mixtape menu. Turn on **Mixtape in Games list** and Mixtape shows up in Games as its own system, so you can add it to Favorites and open it from the home screen. It's off until you turn it on, and turning it off removes it again.
- Updating Mixtape itself has moved into the same SELECT menu.
